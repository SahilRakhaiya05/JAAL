01 How it works

Four steps, and the first two never leave your phone.

JAAL reads what already arrives: a WhatsApp Web message, a Gmail link, a downloaded file, the UPI ID you just copied. Most of it is discarded on the device in under 1 ms.

  1. 01

    Sensors

    Chrome extension, Android app, this page and a Telegram bot see messages, links, downloads and pastes.

  2. 02

    On-device triage

    16 signals score the text locally. Identifiers are hashed; only an 8-character prefix is compared first.

  3. 03

    The graph

    A matched prefix asks the shared graph: is this 1 or 2 hops from a confirmed ring? Answers in about 150 ms.

  4. 04

    The overlay

    A single card, only when there is something to say. No card means no reports. Every check makes the graph denser.

03 Why a graph

Scammers change numbers in days. They keep payout accounts for months.

A blocklist of numbers is always a week late. The account a ring gets paid into is the slow-moving part, and it links every number, link and app the ring uses. Only a graph sees that.

3–7 daystypical life of a scam phone number
90+ daysthe same payout account stays in use
2 hopsfrom a new number to a known ring

Six questions only a graph answers

  1. blast_radiusWho else has this account been paid by?Every number and link 2 hops out, in one query.
  2. ring_detectionWhich of these numbers are one ring?Connected components on hard links, then communities on weighted ones.
  3. money_chokepointWhich account collects from the most numbers?The single report a bank can act on.
  4. lookalike_domainIs sbi-kyc-update.in a copy of a real bank?3-shingle overlap against known domains.
  5. script_similarityIs this the same script with a new number?Message vectors, so a reworded SMS still links.
  6. spread_mapWhich city does it reach next?First-seen order across reports, by day.

Runs on TigerGraph in production: 12 vertex types, 9 edge types, 6 GSQL queries. A local in-memory engine mirrors the schema 1:1.
